About

I’m an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate from JKUAT, based in Kenya, with hands-on experience across power systems, IT operations and software development. At Kenya Power I spent a year as a Systems Designer, validating sites, designing electrical distribution networks and preparing cost estimates and material schedules, including work on the Last Mile programme that extends electricity to rural communities. Earlier, an attachment at the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority at Wilson Airport gave me experience with servers, networking equipment and the systems that support air traffic control. Alongside engineering, I trained as a software developer through Moringa School and the Power Learn Project, and I build web systems that connect to real hardware. I enjoy solving practical problems, explaining technical things clearly, and learning new tools quickly.

Community and Volunteering

I enjoy helping people get unstuck, and volunteering is where I’ve practised that most. From June to September 2025 I was a mentor and learner-support volunteer with the Power Learn Project, where I helped software learners work through technical problems until they were resolved, find their way around the learning platform and reach the resources they needed. Being patient and explaining the same idea in different ways for different people taught me that good technical support is as much about listening as knowing the answer.


I also volunteered with the Fallohide Africa team at the launch of ArGO, an augmented reality mobile game set in Oloolua Forest, Nairobi, with launch funding from WWF-Kenya. I set up devices, helped children use the app, guided groups and coordinated participants as children explored the forest through AR experiences about conservation. Watching young people learn about nature through technology showed me how well digital tools can work for people who aren’t technical.


Both experiences taught me to stay calm and friendly when things get busy and to keep the person I’m helping at the centre.
